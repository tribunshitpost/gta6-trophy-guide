      Roadmap to the Platinum

      About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. The story takes 60–80 hours; the 100% checklist after the credits is the long pole, at about 100 hours. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off on a trophy run.
