Read this first: the one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The Chapter 1 balcony meditation (Lucia, at Jason’s house, on a morning after the mission Down to Earth) is gone once Chapter 1 ends, and there is no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. Everything else can be earned after the story.

Roadmap to the Platinum

About 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no online trophies. Work the phases in order; the checklist in phase 5 is what finally unlocks A Long Road and the Platinum.
